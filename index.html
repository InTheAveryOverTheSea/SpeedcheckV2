        How to Use SpeedCheck

        1. Click "Start" to begin timing your run.

        2. Click "Split" each time you reach a split point in your run. You can also press the spacebar to split, this is recommended, as you don't have to look away from your run

        3. Click "Save Run" to save your current run and splits.

        4. View your personal bests and overall best run in the respective sections.

        5. Use "Modify Splits" to add or remove split points as needed.

        6. Export your runs and personal bests using the "Export Runs & PBs" button.

        7. Clear all saved data with the "Clear Saved Data" button if desired.
