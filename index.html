        How to Use SpeedCheck

        1. Click "Start" to begin timing your run.

        2. Click "Split" to record a split time, you can also press the spacebar to split so you don't have to look at the screen during your run.

        3. Click "Save Run" to save your current run and splits.

        4. View your personal bests/"golds" and overall best run in the section below your run.

        5. Use the "Modify Splits" button to add or remove splits as needed.

        6. Export your runs and PBs or clear saved data using the buttons at the bottom.
